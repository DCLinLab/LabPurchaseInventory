Privacy policy

How information moves through LabPurchaseBot, and the choices available to you.

1. Who operates the app

LabPurchaseBot is a lab-operated assistant maintained by the Lin Lab project operator for invited lab members. Questions about the app or this policy can be sent to linjhumse@gmail.com. This policy covers the bot and its informational website. It does not replace the policies of Google, Slack, GitHub, or OpenAI.

2. Information the bot accesses

Slack messages and package photos

In configured channels, the bot receives human messages, captions, related conversation context, user and channel identifiers, timestamps, and supported attached photos. It can process messages without being mentioned. Labels and photos may contain names, addresses, tracking numbers, product details, and other visible information. Avoid posting unnecessary personal information or secrets.

Order emails from Gmail

The bot reads emails selected by configured sender and date filters, including message headers, subjects, message text, supported PDF and image attachments, and extracted order and shipment details. Forwarded content can include the original correspondence and contact information.

The Google permission gmail.readonly permits reading the authorized mailbox. The sender and date filters are application settings, not a narrower Google permission. The bot does not send email, delete messages, or change mailbox labels.

Google Drive and Sheets

The drive.file permission covers files created by, or explicitly authorized for use with, the app. The bot reads and updates its designated Google spreadsheet for orders, package receipts, and inventory records. These records may include product identifiers, quantities, suppliers, requesters, shipping details, storage locations, and source references.

Connection and operational information

The app stores authorization credentials, processing status, error and activity records, and identifiers used to avoid recording the same message, email, or receipt twice.

3. How information is used and shared

The bot uses information to recognize order updates, read package labels, match deliveries to known orders, maintain shared records, answer lab questions, and diagnose processing problems.

  • OpenAI: Relevant email content, attachment text and images, package photos, Slack messages and conversation context, and selected spreadsheet records or query results are sent to OpenAI Codex for AI-assisted interpretation and responses. The bot uses the operator’s signed-in account. Information is therefore processed outside the lab workstation.
  • Google: Gmail provides source messages; Google Sheets stores the shared order, inventory, and receipt records.
  • Slack: The bot posts order updates, receipt summaries, and query answers to configured channels or threads. People with access to those destinations can see the replies. Spreadsheet collaborators can see the records allowed by their file permissions.
  • The lab operator: The operator administers the workstation and integrations and may review records when assisting with corrections or investigating processing and security problems. Request support only for information you are authorized to share.

LabPurchaseBot does not sell user data or use it for advertising. The bot does not implement a model-training workflow. OpenAI’s retention and any use of submitted content depend on the service terms and the operator’s account data controls; this draft does not promise that provider-side retention or training is disabled.

Google API information is intended only for the disclosed lab-facing functions. The operator must ensure that its use and transfer comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements. Sharing with AI services and lab members must be covered by the account holder’s informed authorization.

4. Storage, protection, and retention

The lab workstation keeps downloaded package photos, captured email content and attachments, extracted records, query records, and processing logs. Google Sheets and Slack retain the records and messages written to them. OpenAI may retain submitted content under the applicable account settings and service terms.

Google authorization tokens are encrypted for the workstation’s Windows user with Windows credential protection. Other local app configuration, caches, photos, and email files are not all encrypted by the application. Their protection also depends on workstation access controls and any operating-system or disk encryption configured by the operator. Credentials and lab records are excluded from the intended public website content.

There is currently no automatic retention or deletion schedule. Local records remain until the operator removes them. Copies in Slack, Google, provider systems, or backups may have separate retention rules. Removing one copy does not remove all copies.

5. Access, correction, deletion, and revocation

Contact linjhumse@gmail.com to ask about access, correct a record, request deletion, or stop processing. Identify the relevant record without sending passwords or access tokens. The operator may need to confirm your identity and authority before acting and coordinate deletion across the workstation, spreadsheet, and Slack. Deletion requests are handled manually; immediate deletion of every provider or backup copy cannot be promised.

The connected Google account holder can remove the app through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not erase information already copied. A Slack administrator can remove the app or ask the operator to disable a channel. Stopping the bot also stops new processing while it is stopped.

6. This informational website

These pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, advertising, or app-managed cookies. They do not receive lab messages, emails, or inventory records. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles website requests and may process technical information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending an email is subject to the destination service’s practices.

7. Changes to this policy

The operator will update the date on this page when the policy changes and notify affected users of material changes through the lab’s communication channel. New uses of Google data require updated disclosures and any required consent before that new use begins.
