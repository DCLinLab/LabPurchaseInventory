Terms of use

A few ground rules for this personal lab project.

Effective September 20, 2026

1. Purpose and access

LabPurchaseBot is a personal, non-commercial project used by its maintainer and a small group of known lab colleagues. It helps us track orders and package receipts using Slack, Gmail, and Google Sheets. There is no public signup, subscription, or paid service. The “operator” in these terms means the project maintainer. Contact linjhumse@gmail.com.

Only connect accounts and share data that you are authorized to use. Account holders and workspace administrators control integration permissions. Using the bot does not grant permission to access another person’s account or restricted records.

2. What the bot does

The bot reads configured order emails, interprets package photos and related messages, matches evidence to shared records, and answers questions. It may process messages in configured channels without an @mention. Production workflows may automatically update the designated spreadsheet and post Slack messages.

Questions about records are read-only. Configured test channels can preview package processing without changing production inventory; their query answers can still use real records. The bot does not place orders, authorize purchases, make payments, or send email.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Provide clear photos and accurate descriptions. State how many packages arrived when the count cannot be determined from the photos.
  • Share only information needed for the lab task. Do not post passwords, access tokens, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information.
  • Review important identifiers, quantities, matches, and status before relying on them. Report a suspected duplicate or incorrect record to the operator.
  • Respect access permissions and use the bot only for authorized lab work. Do not attempt to extract private information, override safeguards, or disrupt the service.

4. Accuracy and inventory limits

AI interpretation and order matching can be wrong or incomplete. Missing information is not proof that an item or order does not exist. A shipment notification does not establish physical receipt by the lab.

Recorded receipt quantities describe deliveries; they do not automatically account for consumption, loss, transfers, or disposal. They therefore may not represent stock currently on hand. Use source documents and a physical check when accuracy matters.

The bot’s replies are operational assistance, not approval to purchase or a substitute for the lab’s purchasing, financial, safety, or compliance procedures.

5. Privacy and external services

Read the Privacy policy before connecting an account or posting information for the bot to process. Relevant content is sent to OpenAI for AI processing, and information is stored locally and in the connected services. Authorized channel members and spreadsheet collaborators may see the resulting records and replies.

Google, Slack, OpenAI, and GitHub operate under their own terms and policies. The operator must configure and use these services consistently with its commitments to users and applicable service requirements.

6. Availability and changes

Operation depends on the lab workstation, network access, account authorization, AI usage availability, and third-party services. Processing may be delayed or unavailable. The operator may change, suspend, or discontinue features or access and will communicate material changes to affected lab users when practical.

This is a personal project maintained as time permits. Please keep the original order and delivery records and do not rely on the bot as the only source of information.

7. Stopping use and requesting help

You can stop interacting with the bot, ask the operator to disable processing, or revoke permissions for an account you control. These actions do not automatically delete existing records; deletion and correction requests follow the Privacy policy.

Questions, access requests, and record corrections: linjhumse@gmail.com. Updated terms will carry a new date, and material changes will be communicated through the lab’s communication channel.
