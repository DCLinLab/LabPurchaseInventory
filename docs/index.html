01 / Track

Follow order updates

The bot reads configured order emails from the connected Gmail account and reports relevant status changes in the designated Slack channel.

02 / Receive

Post a package label

Upload a clear photo in a configured channel. Include the number of packages when several identical packages arrive. The bot reads the label, looks for a matching order, and records the receipt when the evidence supports it.

03 / Ask

Ask in everyday language

Ask about orders, recent arrivals, or recorded inventory. The bot uses the available records to answer and asks for clarification when needed.

Know what you connect

Your lab data,
with a clear purpose.

LabPurchaseBot is maintained by the Lin Lab project operator for a limited group of lab members. It runs on a lab-controlled workstation and connects to authorized Slack channels, Gmail, and a designated Google spreadsheet.

AI processing uses OpenAI Codex. Relevant messages, email content, attachments, label photos, and spreadsheet records may be sent to OpenAI to interpret them and answer questions. Processing is not entirely local.

The bot can read human messages and photos in configured channels without an @mention. Production receipt and email workflows can update the spreadsheet and post replies. Record questions are read-only.

See data access, storage, and deletion details →

A helpful record, with limits.

AI can misread a label or miss an order match. Verify important quantities, identifiers, and status before acting. A shipping update does not prove that the lab received a package. Recorded deliveries are cumulative; remaining stock may be different because consumption is not automatically tracked.

The bot does not place orders or send email. This site does not provide access to lab records.
