LabPurchaseBot

A small tool I use with my lab colleagues to keep track of orders and the packages we receive.

This is a personal, non-commercial project for the maintainer and a small group of people they know in the lab. There is no public signup, subscription, or paid service.

What it does

  • Reads selected order emails from the connected Gmail account and posts relevant shipping updates in our Slack channel.
  • Reads package-label photos posted in a configured channel, looks for a matching order, and records deliveries in our Google spreadsheet. For several identical packages, include the count with the photo.
  • Answers questions about our order and inventory records in Slack.

It can process messages without an @mention. It does not place orders, make payments, or send email.

Where the information goes

The bot runs on a lab workstation. It uses Gmail, Slack, Google Sheets, and OpenAI Codex. Relevant email content, attachments, package photos, messages, and spreadsheet records may be sent to OpenAI to read the information and answer questions.

Records are stored on the workstation and in the connected services. Replies are visible to people in the relevant Slack channel or thread; spreadsheet access follows the file’s sharing permissions. This website only explains the project and does not display our lab records.

The Privacy policy explains access, storage, sharing, and deletion in more detail.

A few practical limits

The bot can misread a label or match the wrong order, so check important details. An email saying “shipped” does not mean we have received the package. Recorded deliveries also do not tell us how much remains after supplies have been used.

The bot depends on the workstation and connected services being available. Questions about records are read-only; order updates and package receipts can change the spreadsheet.
